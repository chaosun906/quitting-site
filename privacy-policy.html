隐私政策

Quitting · 戒了吧  |  最近更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日

简而言之：我们不收集你的任何数据。所有信息只保存在你自己的手机上。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

Quitting（戒了吧）是一款纯本地应用。我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何用户数据，包括但不限于：

  • 不要求注册或登录，不存在账号信息
  • 不包含任何统计、分析或广告 SDK
  • 不访问网络，没有服务器
  • 不请求位置、通讯录、相册、麦克风、摄像头等任何系统权限
  • 不使用 Cookie 或任何追踪技术

2. 数据存储

你创建的戒断目标、计时进度、破戒记录等全部信息，仅保存在你的设备本地。这些数据不会离开你的手机，我们（开发者）无法查看、获取或同步它们。

请注意：如果你删除本应用或抹除设备数据，本地存储的目标与记录将一并被删除，且无法恢复。

3. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息（实际上，我们不收集任何人的信息）。

4. 第三方服务

本应用不接入任何第三方服务，因此不存在向第三方共享数据的行为。

5. 政策变更

如果未来应用功能发生变化并涉及数据收集，我们将在本页面更新政策并修改"最近更新"日期。

6. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：793783562@qq.com

Privacy Policy

Quitting  |  Last updated: September 24, 2026

In short: we do not collect any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Quitting is a fully offline app. We do not collect, store, or transmit any user data, including but not limited to:

  • No sign-up or login is required — there are no accounts
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are embedded
  • The app makes no network requests and has no servers
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other system permission
  • No cookies or tracking technologies of any kind

2. Data Storage

All information you create — quit goals, timers, relapse records — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your phone, and we (the developer) cannot view, access, or sync it.

Please note: if you delete the app or erase your device, locally stored goals and records will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

3. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (in fact, we collect nothing from anyone).

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, so no data is shared with third parties.

5. Changes to This Policy

If future updates to the app introduce data collection, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: 793783562@qq.com
