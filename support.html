Quitting Support

Quit bad habits, one day at a time. Quitting is a private, offline habit-cessation tracker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your goals and records are stored only on your device. We do not collect, upload, or share any personal data. No account is required.

How does the penalty work?

When you relapse, the timer resets, milestones re-lock, and the penalty you chose (donation, workout, study…) is recorded in your relapse history so you can stay accountable.

Can I edit milestone rewards?

Yes. On a goal's detail page, tap any milestone to edit its reward at any time.

Why was I charged $0.99?

Quitting is a one-time paid app. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads. All future updates are included.
